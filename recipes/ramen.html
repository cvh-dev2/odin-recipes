World's Best Ramen

Description

No description needed for these noodles.

Ingredients

  • 1 Packet of the World's Greatest Ramen
  • Water
  • Anything else your heart desires

Equipment

  • 1 Bowl of some kind; fancier the better
  • 1 Microwave for this recipe...stove top optional, but not optimal
  • 1 Fork

Steps

  1. Step 1: Gather your ingredients.
  2. Step 2: Open the packet and take out the seasoning. Place brick in the bowl.
  3. Step 3: Add water to the bowl. Place in microwave for what you guess is the right amount of time.
  4. Step 4: Burn hands taking it out of microwave. Add ice cubes to cool down overcooked ramen.
  5. Step 5: Let sit for unknown period of time. Enjoy!
