World's Greatest Cereal

Description

The cereal of the sugar Gods.

Ingredients

  • The world's greatest cereal
  • Milk of the cow

Equipment

  • 1 Bowl, bigger and fancier the better
  • 1 giant spoon

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Pour the right amount of cereal into bowl of choice.
  3. Pour milk of the cow into bowl until satisfied.
  4. Devour the cereal before it gets soggy.
